Membership Plans

Become a member of Readers' Haven and enjoy thousands of books, quiet study spaces and digital resources. Choose the plan that suits you best.

Student Plan

PKR 1,000 / year

  • Borrowing limit: 3 books
  • Loan period: 14 days
  • Late return: PKR 10 per day per book
  • Valid student ID required

Adult Plan

PKR 2,000 / year

  • Borrowing limit: 5 books
  • Loan period: 21 days
  • Late return: PKR 20 per day per book
  • Valid CNIC required

Family Plan

PKR 4,000 / year

  • Borrowing limit: 10 books (up to 4 members)
  • Loan period: 21 days
  • Late return: PKR 30 per day per book
  • Parent or guardian CNIC required

Late-Return Rules

  • Fines are charged per book for every day after the due date.
  • Borrowing is suspended if unpaid fines exceed PKR 500.
  • A book can be renewed once if no one else has reserved it.
  • Lost or damaged books must be replaced or paid for at market price.
