Online Catalogue

Find any book in our collection by its title, author or subject.

Timings: Available online all day, every day.

How to use: Open the catalogue, type the book name, then check if it is available on the shelf.

💻

E-Books

Read digital books and study material on your phone or laptop.

Timings: Monday - Saturday, 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM.

How to use: Visit the help desk, get your access details from the staff and log in to read.

📖

Study Room Booking

Book a calm room for solo study or group work.

Timings: Monday - Saturday, 10:00 AM - 7:00 PM.

How to use: Go to the reception, tell them your preferred time and get your slot confirmed.

🖨️

Printing & Scanning

Get your assignments printed or scan your notes and documents.

Timings: Monday - Friday, 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM.

How to use: Carry your file on a USB, give it to the service desk and pay the small fee.

📶

Free Wi-Fi

Free internet for research, online classes and learning.

Timings: Same as library opening hours.

How to use: Ask the staff for the password and connect to the library network.
