Our Library Services

Everything you need for reading, learning and discovery.

What We Offer

📚

Online Catalogue

Search for books by title, author or category.

How to use: Open the catalogue, enter a book name and check its availability.

💻

E-Books

Access digital reading materials and selected e-books.

How to use: Ask the library staff for access to available digital resources.

📖

Study Room Booking

Reserve a quiet space for individual or group study.

How to use: Contact the reception desk to request a booking.

🖨️

Printing & Scanning

Print documents and scan important study materials.

How to use: Bring your digital document to the service desk.

📶

Free Wi-Fi

Use internet access for research and educational activities.

How to use: Request the Wi-Fi access details from library staff.
